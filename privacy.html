Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Overview

Cosmic Merge is developed by Smidley. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

Cosmic Merge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks of any kind.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievement progress are shared with Apple's Game Center service. This is managed entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Cosmic Merge does not access your Game Center profile information beyond what is needed to submit scores and achievements.

Local Data

Game settings (sound, music, and haptic preferences) and your high score are stored locally on your device using standard system storage. This data never leaves your device.

Device Sensors

The app uses your device's accelerometer (CoreMotion) to adjust the lighting on celestial objects based on tilt. This sensor data is processed locally in real time and is never recorded or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Cosmic Merge does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, ad networks, or external services beyond Apple's Game Center.

Children's Privacy

Cosmic Merge does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no external links within gameplay.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at smidley@gmail.com.
