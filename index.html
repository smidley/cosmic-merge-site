Cosmic Merge

Drop, Merge & Build the Stars

A physics-based puzzle game where you drop celestial objects, chain combos, and evolve cosmic dust into black holes.

11 Celestial Tiers
Evolve from dust particles to black holes. Each tier features a unique procedural surface that reacts to your device's tilt.
Combo Chains
Merge quickly to trigger multipliers that skyrocket your score. The deeper the combo, the bigger the reward.
Real-Time Shaders
Flowing lava, crackling lightning, bioluminescent oceans, and swirling accretion disks — all rendered in real time.
Game Center
Compete on global leaderboards and unlock 13 achievements across tier milestones, score goals, and combo challenges.
Physics-Driven
Realistic gravity, momentum, and collisions mean every game plays out differently. One drop can trigger a cascade.
No Ads. No IAP.
Just the game. Plays on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.
